Print pieces

Every PDF is print-ready: full color, sharp vector text, and 0.125" bleed on every edge so the color runs to the trim.

Two ways to print
  1. FedEx Office (fastest): order online at office.fedex.com, upload the PDF and pick up at any store, often the same day. The steps are under each piece below.
  2. Through Real: Real doesn't publish a print shop, so check the marketing resources in reZEN or ask your Real broker or agent support whether they offer print ordering or discounts. Send them the same PDFs. They're already sized, with bleed.
Not sure about a setting? Take the PDF to the FedEx counter and ask for a printed proof first. It's cheap and catches surprises.

Postcard mailer

8.5" x 5.5" · Double-sided · mail to your sphere or hand out at events

Download print-ready PDF

How to print it
  1. Go to office.fedex.com and choose Postcards.
  2. Size 5.5" x 8.5", landscape, double-sided.
  3. Paper: 100 lb matte cover (suits the ink-drawing look) or 100 lb gloss.
  4. Upload postcard.pdf (page 1 = front, page 2 = back).
  5. Quantity: start with 100. Check the preview, then order for pickup.

Mailing tip: USPS postcard rates only cover cards up to 4.25" x 6", so this size mails at the First-Class letter rate. Address it in the open area on the right of the back.

Historic-home flyer

8.5" x 11" · Single-sided · open houses, listing appointments, leave-behinds

Download print-ready PDF

How to print it
  1. Choose Flyers at office.fedex.com.
  2. Size 8.5" x 11", single-sided, full color.
  3. Paper: 100 lb gloss text, or 80 lb cover if you want it to feel more substantial.
  4. Upload flyer.pdf. Quantity: 25 to 50 per open house.

Keep a few in your car for walkthroughs of older homes. It's a natural conversation starter.

Thank-you note cards

4.25" x 5.5" · Double-sided · handwritten thank-yous to clients and referral partners

Download print-ready PDF

How to print it
  1. Choose Postcards at office.fedex.com.
  2. Size 4.25" x 5.5", portrait, double-sided.
  3. Paper: matte (important: you can write on matte with a regular pen; gloss smears).
  4. Upload notecard.pdf. Quantity: 50.
  5. Buy A2 envelopes (4.375" x 5.75") to mail them.

The back stays mostly blank so you can write your note by hand.
